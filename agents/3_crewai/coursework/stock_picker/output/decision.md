# Companies not selected and why

## Apple
Apple is a high-quality business with elite brand strength, ecosystem lock-in, and strong services revenue. However, it was not selected because:
- Its near-term growth depends heavily on the success of the **foldable iPhone**, which is a premium, niche launch at a **$1,999 starting price**.
- That makes adoption uncertain and likely limited initially to affluent early adopters.
- Apple is still an excellent defensive mega-cap, but the upside appears more incremental than transformational right now.

**Bottom line:** Apple is durable, but Alphabet offers a stronger mix of growth and monetization opportunity.

## Nvidia
Nvidia remains the dominant AI infrastructure leader and has exceptional upside. It was not selected because:
- Expectations and valuation are already extremely elevated.
- The stock is highly sensitive to AI capex cycles, export controls, customer concentration, and potential normalization in spending.
- The upside is huge, but the risk profile is also much more cyclical and sentiment-driven.

**Bottom line:** Nvidia may have the highest upside in some scenarios, but Alphabet is the better risk-adjusted choice.

## CrowdStrike
CrowdStrike is a strong cybersecurity platform and a compelling AI-security beneficiary. It was not selected because:
- It is more sensitive to valuation and sentiment swings.
- Cybersecurity remains competitive, with pressure from Microsoft, Palo Alto Networks, Zscaler, and others.
- The investment case is strong, but it is narrower and more volatility-prone than Alphabet.

**Bottom line:** CrowdStrike is attractive, but Alphabet is more diversified and less dependent on one enterprise security theme.

## OpenAI
OpenAI is influential in AI, but it was not selected because:
- It is **not a publicly traded company**, so it is not a straightforward public-market investment.
- It faces elevated governance, safety, and cyber-risk concerns.
- There is less transparency than with public equities.

**Bottom line:** OpenAI is strategically important, but it is not the right answer for this investment selection task.

## Marvell Technology
Marvell has meaningful exposure to AI networking and custom silicon, but it was not selected because:
- It is more execution-sensitive and less diversified than larger platform names.
- It is heavily tied to AI infrastructure spending and hyperscaler demand.
- The stock can be volatile when AI sentiment weakens.

**Bottom line:** Marvell is promising, but Alphabet has a stronger and more durable investment profile.

## Intel
Intel was not selected because:
- The turnaround is still incomplete.
- It faces major competition from AMD, Nvidia, TSMC, and others.
- Execution risk remains high across manufacturing, product roadmap, and foundry ambitions.

**Bottom line:** Intel is a turnaround story, not the best current investment.

## Broadcom
Broadcom is one of the strongest AI infrastructure winners and has a good combination of semiconductors and software. It was not selected because:
- It carries notable **customer concentration risk**.
- Its AI thesis is still highly dependent on sustained hyperscaler spending.
- While excellent, the business is more exposed to a few large relationships than Alphabet.

**Bottom line:** Broadcom is a top-tier company, but Alphabet has a more balanced long-term profile.
